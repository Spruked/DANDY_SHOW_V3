How ORB Weaver Works

Scan. Compile. Deploy.

Three stages. One complete Site Intelligence package, ready for any ORB-powered AI agent.

Step 01
SCAN
ORB Weaver dispatches 220+ scan types across every layer of a target website — structure, navigation, UI elements, accessibility, content, flows, brand, schema, and more. Nothing is left undiscovered. Every control, route, pointer target, and workflow is mapped.
Step 02
COMPILE
All scan data is normalized, deduplicated, cross-referenced, and compiled into a Site World — a structured knowledge graph, pointer manifest, route capsule set, and workflow map. Fully versioned, timestamped, and confidence-scored.
Step 03
DEPLOY
The compiled ORB intelligence package is delivered as a structured deployment artifact. Any ORB-powered AI receives complete site knowledge instantly — no guessing, no custom coding, no integration work.
From blank website to fully ORB-ready in a single scan run.
