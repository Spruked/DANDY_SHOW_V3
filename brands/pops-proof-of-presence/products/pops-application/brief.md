POPS (Proof of Presence System) is a family of desktop, Android, and iOS applications designed to help parents protect their records, document their involvement, and preserve the facts when family relationships become complicated.

POPS provides a practical system for recording important events, documenting parenting-time exchanges, organizing communications, preserving photographs and documents, and building chronological records of parental involvement.

The desktop application serves as a central workspace for organizing evidence, reviewing timelines, managing case information, and preparing documentation. The mobile applications bring evidence capture into everyday situations, allowing parents to document events when and where they occur.

Built around privacy, accountability, and evidence integrity, POPS emphasizes local control, original-record preservation, and a clear distinction between allegations, observations, and supporting evidence.

POPS exists because being present and being able to prove your presence are not always the same thing. Its purpose is to give parents practical tools to protect the record, preserve the truth, and demonstrate their efforts to remain involved in their children's lives.
